# Romans — visual redesign

> **Status (2026-08-10):** the rail-and-bead design (seven stops on a left
> rail, a per-verse bead track along the bottom) is retired; this doc records
> why and what replaced it. The new design is per-chapter full-frame scenes
> threaded by the **Romans Road** milestones. Old design preserved in
> `backups/romans_rail/`.

## What was wrong with the rail design

Rendered review (2026-08-10) confirmed four problems:

1. **The left rail was a static table of contents.** A quarter of the frame
   never changed across 433 verses.
2. **The bead track was a verse counter.** It moved every verse, but what it
   said was only "you are on verse 23 of 31" — motion without meaning.
3. **The imagery stagnated.** One set-piece held for whole multi-chapter
   spans: ASCENT for chapters 6–8 (87 verses), TREE for 9–11, BODY for 12–16.
4. **Chapters 6–8 literally read as a line graph** — a polyline climbing
   left-to-right over a dashed baseline. The most theological stretch of the
   letter looked like a stock chart.

And one missed opportunity: **the Romans Road** — the classic evangelistic
walk through 3:23 → 5:8 → 6:23 → 10:9 → 10:13 — was nowhere, in the one book
that owns it.

## The new design

**One full-frame scene per chapter** (two for ch. 1 and ch. 3, which turn
hard mid-chapter). No rail, no bead track, **no per-verse caption word** —
nothing on the canvas counts the verses. Per-verse change lives inside the
drawing: something is drawn, lit, or advanced on nearly every verse, under
the house draw-once-then-hold rule. Chapter + verse sit small in the
top-left corner as plain text.

**Text budget, revised for this book.** Romans argues; a wordless canvas
fights the text instead of serving it. The budget is widened from
"names and numbers only" to also allow **short engraved phrases** —
2–7 words, WEB wording or a tight paraphrase, ALL-CAPS, carved into a stone
or set as a banner — at the theological hinge verses only (BUT NOW /
NO CONDEMNATION / the milestone texts). Never sentences, never captions,
never per-verse prose. Roughly one phrase per 10–15 verses.

**The Romans Road thread.** A Roman road (flagstone perspective, verge
stones, distant arch) is the connective scene the letter keeps returning to.
Each classic Road verse plants an engraved milestone; each return shows the
milestones already planted, so the road accumulates:

| Frame | Verses    | Milestone                                     |
|-------|-----------|-----------------------------------------------|
| I     | 3:23      | ALL HAVE SINNED                               |
| II    | 5:6–11    | CHRIST DIED FOR US (cross on the hill beyond) |
| III   | 6:23      | WAGES OF SIN · DEATH / GIFT OF GOD · LIFE     |
| IV    | 10:8–13   | CONFESS · BELIEVE, then WHOEVER WILL CALL —   |
|       |           | the road reaches the arch                     |

The stone of stumbling laid in Zion (9:32–33) is drawn **on the same road**,
tying Israel's stumble to the walk without a full road frame.

## The sixteen scenes

1. **The road to Rome / the darkening** — Paul's letter sets out on the road
   toward a distant Rome arch; the road draws further each verse; banner at
   16–17 (THE POWER OF GOD, v16). At v18 the sky lowers: the exchange —
   glory traded for images; the list verses (24–32) accumulate red marks in
   the dark.
2. **The impartial judge** — the bema; the accuser's pointing arm arcs back
   at himself (v1); law tablet and conscience-heart weighed the same;
   v29 the heart, not the flesh, is circled.
3. **Every mouth stopped → BUT NOW** — the assembled dock of ch. 1–2 falls
   silent (mouths are lines that close, 9–19); v21 light breaks in from
   above: BUT NOW; v23 Road frame I; vv24–26 the mercy-seat under radiating
   light.
4. **Abraham's stars** — the promise counted out: stars kindle verse by
   verse; COUNTED written across the sky at v3; the seal-ring at v11; one
   near star flares FOR US at vv23–25.
5. **Peace, and the two doors** — the stair of 1–5 (peace, access, hope,
   love); Road frame II at 6–11; from v12 two doorways: Adam's, a gray
   procession passing under a lintel that darkens; Christ's, gold and wider
   (MUCH MORE, v15/v20).
6. **Buried and raised** — the waterline; the figure goes down into the
   grave-pool with Christ and rises on the far side (3–11); shackles break
   (6–7, 17–18); members presented as instruments (13); Road frame III
   at v23.
7. **The divided man** — one figure, two pulls: a cord up to GOD'S LAW
   (the inward man delights), a chain down to SIN in the members; the
   marriage release of 1–6 as two rings, one opened; v24 WHO WILL DELIVER
   ME? — the up-cord alone holds.
8. **No condemnation → more than conquerors** — the GUILTY stamp of ch. 3
   shattered (v1, NO CONDEMNATION); ABBA at 15; creation strains at the
   horizon (19–22); the five-link golden chain arcs overhead, link per beat
   (29–30); the finale rings the figure in light while named arrows
   (SWORD, FAMINE…) break against it — MORE THAN CONQUERORS (31–39).
9. **The potter** — the wheel, the one lump, two vessels; the chosen line
   (Isaac not Ishmael, Jacob not Esau) at 6–13; the remnant at 27; at 32–33
   a stone is laid **on the road**: the stumbling stone.
10. **The word is near** — the road, held for the whole chapter; Road
    frame IV: CONFESS · BELIEVE at v9, the arch reached at v13 (WHOEVER
    WILL CALL); then the road runs outward past the arch and messengers'
    feet carry the word away (HOW BEAUTIFUL, 14–15); hands stretched out
    all day at v21.
11. **The olive tree** — kept from the old design (its one good set-piece)
    and refined: 7000 reserved, branches broken, wild shoots grafted, ALL
    ISRAEL at 26; the doxology (33–36) rings the tree in widening circles —
    OF HIM · THROUGH HIM · TO HIM.
12. **The living sacrifice** — a figure standing whole on the altar (v1,
    A LIVING SACRIFICE); the mind re-formed (v2); the one body with its
    gift-flames (4–8); burning coals (v20); OVERCOME EVIL WITH GOOD (v21).
13. **The night is far spent** — the sword and the tribute compact (1–7);
    the commandments folding into LOVE (8–10); then the horizon: night
    giving way to dawn, and armour traced in light on the waking figure
    (11–14, THE ARMOUR OF LIGHT).
14. **One table** — weak and strong at one long table, herbs at one seat, a
    full plate at another; days marked alike above (5–6); the head of the
    table is the judgment seat (10–12, EVERY KNEE); the stumbling block
    lifted away (13); RIGHTEOUSNESS · PEACE · JOY over the table (17).
15. **Jerusalem round to Spain** — the mission map: the arc Jerusalem →
    Illyricum → Rome → Spain; the collection carried back before the push
    west (25–28); the four OT voices of 9–12 rise as the Gentiles' HOPE.
14b. **The doxology, early (WEB 14:24–26)** — the WEB follows the
    manuscripts that place the closing doxology at the end of chapter 14
    rather than at 16:25–27, so WEB Romans 14 runs to **26 verses** while
    every other version stops at 23. Those three verses are drawn to rhyme
    with the chapter-16 treatment of the same text: the mystery sealed under
    a stamp (v24), the roll opened and shining (v25), then widening rings of
    glory over the quiet table (v26). `CH_LEN` therefore carries the
    **maximum** verse count across all translations, not one version's — a
    version simply never requests the verses it does not have.
16. **The lamps of Rome** — the city as clustered houses; Phebe arrives
    bearing the letter (1–2); every greeting lights a lamp (3–16, names
    labelled as lit); the crooked path avoided (17); Satan under foot (20);
    the doxology fuses the lamps into one glow (25–27, TO GOD, ALONE WISE).

## Rules carried forward

* Draw once, then hold — `since(pos, ch, v)` gates every element; a thing
  animates only on the verse it first appears. **This applies to growth as
  well as appearance**: the chapter-1 road lays a new stretch each verse and
  only that stretch draws itself in (`draw_road(..., grow_from=)`), never the
  whole road again.
* Highlight = pulse outline, never redraw.
* **Every box is measured, never estimated.** `text_w()` reads real Georgia
  advance widths (via PIL) so rings, chips and the engraved milestone faces
  are cut to fit their text. A characters-times-a-guess estimate is what
  clipped the first cut's stone faces.
* WEB wording (or tight paraphrase) for every on-canvas phrase; one set of
  files for all translations (`translation_suffixed: false`).
* Palette: the tabernacle palette plus OLIVE/WILD (kept) and WATER for
  chapter 6.

## Automated checks

`scratchpad/audit.py` (dev-only) parses all 433 generated SVGs and reports
overlapping text boxes, text wider than its containing box, text off-canvas,
and verses whose drawing is byte-identical to the previous verse. The design
target is **zero** of the first three and **no identical run longer than
three verses** — a hold of two or three across tightly-argued verses is the
draw-once-then-hold rule working, not stagnation. Current state: 0 / 0 / 0,
longest run 3.
